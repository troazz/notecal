;; Division by a quantity with a unit keeps the divisor's number and unit together
8 km / 2 km => 4
1 GiB / 4 KiB => 262,144
100 km / 2 h => 50 km / h
1,000 m / 250 m => 4
3 hours / 30 minutes => 6
10 kg / 2 kg => 5
1 TB / 500 GB => 2
;; A bare-number divisor keeps the unit
10 km / 2 => 5 km
90 minutes / 3 => 30 minutes
;; A bare-number dividend keeps fend's reading
10 / 2 m => 5 m
;; Explicit parentheses always win
(8 km) / (2 km) => 4
8 km / (2 km) => 4
---
distance = 420 km => 420 km
time = 6 hours => 6 hours
distance / time => 70 km / hour
distance / 2 km => 210
distance / 2 => 210 km
