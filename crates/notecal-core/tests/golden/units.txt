100 kph in mph => 62.137119 mph
60 mph in km/h => 96.56064 km / h
20 °C in °F => 68 °F
20 C in F => 68 °F
0 °C in K => 273.15 K
1 acre in m^2 => 4,046.856422 m^2
100 m^2 in ft^2 => 1,076.391042 ft^2
1 l in ml => 1,000 ml
1 kg in lb => 2.204623 lbs
3 days 4 hours in minutes => 4,560 minutes

1 week in days => 7 days

1 km + 500 m => 1.5 km
1 GiB in MiB => 1,024 MiB
1 TB in GB => 1,000 GB

;; Division by a quantity with a unit: the divisor's number and unit stay together.

1 GiB / 4 KiB => 262,144
100 km / 2 h => 50 km / h
1,000 m / 250 m => 4
;; A bare-number dividend keeps fend's reading: the unit applies to the result.
10 / 2 m => 5 m
10 km / 2 => 5 km
